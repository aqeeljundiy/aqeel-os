aqeelOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

aqeelOS is a private assistant operated by Aqilla Abdurrahman Jundiy (Elkiya, elkiya.com) for his own accounts and businesses. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, aqeelOS may access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Calendar and Google Contacts for the owner's own accounts only.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner has asked for, such as preparing reports, reconciling finance sheets, creating document and email drafts, and summarising information. aqeelOS does not send email on its own unless the owner instructs it to.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Data is processed only to provide these features to the owner. aqeelOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the owner, readable only by the owner's system account. Content is kept only as long as needed to complete a task.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
